HomePrivacy Policy

Cypress Gulf Coast Roofing & Gutters LLC ("Cypress," "we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit roofsbycypress.com, contact us, or submit a form through our website or our ads on Facebook and Instagram, and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Information you give us: your name, phone number, email address, property address or ZIP code, and details about your roof or project when you call us, email us, or fill out a form (including lead forms on Facebook and Instagram).
  • Information collected automatically: basic technical data such as your browser type, device, pages visited, and how you arrived at our site, collected through cookies and similar tools.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your request, schedule your free roof inspection or estimate, and provide our services.
  • To contact you by phone, text message, or email about your request.
  • To improve our website, services, and advertising.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Text Messages and Calls

If you give us your phone number, we may call or text you about your inquiry. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP to any text message to opt out, or ask us at any time to stop contacting you.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run our business (for example, our CRM, scheduling, and marketing providers), with insurance companies or adjusters when you ask us to help with a claim, or when required by law.

Advertising and Cookies

We advertise on platforms such as Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and Google. These platforms may use cookies or similar technologies to show our ads and measure their results. You can manage ad preferences in your Facebook, Instagram, and Google account settings and through your browser's cookie controls.

Data Security and Retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information and keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

Your Choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you, by using the contact details below.

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last changed.
